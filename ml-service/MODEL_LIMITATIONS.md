# ORVIX Recovery Prediction Engine - Model Limitations (v1)

## Overview
The Recovery Prediction Engine estimates the probability of successful revenue recovery given a payment failure context and a candidate recovery action:
$$P(\text{recovery} \mid \text{context}, \text{action})$$
for candidate actions: `RETRY`, `PAYMENT_LINK`, `EMAIL`, `HUMAN_ESCALATION`.

---

## Key Technical & Operational Limitations

### 1. Model Architecture & Linearity (Logistic Regression)
- **Linear Decision Boundary**: Logistic Regression models log-odds as a linear combination of input features. It cannot natively capture non-linear feature interactions (e.g., interaction between high payment amount, specific failure reason, and high attempt count) without explicit cross-feature engineering.
- **Suboptimal for Multi-Modal Distributions**: Complex failure modes (e.g. transient gateway outages vs card expiration vs customer fraud) may exhibit non-monotonic recovery responses.

### 2. Synthetic Data Bias & Simulator Simplifications
- **Synthetic Historical Data**: The model is trained on synthetic historical events generated by the ORVIX simulator. While realistic, synthetic events rely on simplified underlying probabilistic assumptions (e.g., fixed retry/link success rates per failure category).
- **Lack of Real-World Noise**: Real-world variables like time-of-day human behavior, bank holiday processing delays, multi-issuer downtime cascades, and email deliverability rates are simplified.

### 3. Action Independence Assumption
- **Single-Step Prediction**: The model evaluates each candidate action independently for a single step, rather than modeling sequential multi-step policy rollouts or lifetime action trajectories.
- **Absence of Action Wearoff Dynamics**: Repeatedly sending payment links or emails may lower customer response rates due to fatigue, which is not fully captured in single-pass prediction.

### 4. Feature Representation & High-Cardinality Variables
- **OneHotEncoding Overhead**: Categorical variables (`failure_reason`, `payment_method`, `customer_segment`, `action`) use One-Hot Encoding. New unseen failure reason strings at inference time are ignored via `handle_unknown="ignore"`.

---

## Future Roadmap & Enhancements

1. **Gradient Boosted Decision Trees (XGBoost / LightGBM)**: Capture non-linear feature interactions and threshold effects automatically.
2. **Contextual Bandits (Thompson Sampling / UCB1)**: Transition from batch supervised learning to online reinforcement learning for dynamic action exploration vs exploitation.
3. **Sequential Policy Optimization**: Use Markov Decision Processes (MDPs) or Reinforcement Learning to optimize multi-step recovery sequences over time.
